# Icono de Extra Extra

Ilustración generada con la herramienta integrada ImageGen. Original: `assets/newsboy-original.png`. Versiones de producción: `favicon.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon.ico` e `icons/*.png`. La primera propuesta SVG se conserva en `assets/newsboy-first-draft.svg` como historial; no se publica ni se utiliza en la aplicación.

El icono representa a un vendedor de periódicos de principios del siglo XX con boina, rostro visible y dos brazos separados. Uno sostiene el periódico levantado y el otro se apoya junto a la cintura. Las versiones para navegador y dispositivos se derivan del mismo original. El icono adaptable lleva margen adicional para permitir el recorte del sistema.

## Prompt de generación

Create one polished, charming illustrated app icon for a Spanish news reader named Extra Extra. Original character: an early 1900s young newspaper vendor, waist-up in three-quarter profile facing right, wearing a clearly recognizable oversized newsboy flat cap (boina), short jacket and waistcoat. Cheerful face with a small open mouth as if shouting the news. EXACTLY TWO ARMS, anatomically clear and spatially separated: his right arm extends up and to the right, with ONE hand holding ONE folded newspaper above head height; his left arm bends down naturally with hand resting near his waist, well away from the raised arm. Both arms visibly connect to their proper shoulders; no overlapping limbs, no extra hands, no limbs hidden in confusing shapes. The face, cap, raised arm and newspaper must read as a simple iconic silhouette. Professional editorial illustration, tasteful vintage woodcut influence but clean modern flat shapes, confident smooth contours, very few bold interior details, no hatching or textures. Warm ivory skin and newspaper, dark midnight navy for cap and vest, muted terracotta accents on clothes, solid deep terracotta burgundy background #944333. Square composition, subject large, 10 percent clear margin all around, waist cropped cleanly at lower third. Head and cap are prominent and beautifully drawn. The newspaper has just three broad graphic lines and a rectangular image block, absolutely NO readable letters, no typography or labels. No border, no mockup, no gradient, no drop shadow, no decorative sparks. A beautiful distinctive finished logo illustration, not a stick figure. Opaque square image suitable for app icons; do not round off the outer canvas corners.
